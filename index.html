Origins

Fijee (2014–)

Fijee-Project Finite-element forward/inverse EEG solver (Cobigo 2014) after Merlet et al. 2013 (PLoS ONE, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0057330), the Rennes model coupling an FEM head, transcranial stimulation and neural-mass cortical columns back to scalp EEG; the early Center17 effort whose threads now run through neurojax, brain-fwi and ti-sim; C++17/CMake/Docker modernization in progress C++ fijee 2017 integration plan: Fijee forward modelling with FreeSurfer, FSL, TRACULA and MNE via DataLad — infocypher-scieng-deps Build-dependency tracking for Fijee, the seed of the NeuroFedora packaging work —
