About CV Writing

Morgan G. Hough

I'm a neuroimaging researcher and scientific software engineer living in San Francisco, CA. I'm currently the Executive Director of NeuroTechX and the Director and Principal Scientist of Biopunk Lab. I also work on developmental neurobiology with the Orthogonal Research and Education Laboratory.

I recently co-authored A ‘good’ regulator may provide a world model for intelligent systems with Bradly Alicea, Amanda Nelson and Jesse Parent, in Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society A (2026).

I write Spinning Up curricula, each a runnable repository: active inference, PDE solvers, mechanistic interpretability, and OpenScope.

My open-source work with OREL includes controls-and-trajectories, tri-lens, neurosim and SustainHub-jaxmarl. With OpenWorm's DevoWorm group: hgx, devograph and biohub-starter. With Biopunk Lab: anatomical-compiler, biopunk-bioreactor, biopunk-jubilee, biopunk-duckweed, biopunk-artbot, biopunk-display, chimerax-vampnet and chimerax-origami.

I previously worked in the Analysis Group at Oxford's FMRIB Centre, at the Institute of Psychiatry at King's College London, in the Departments of Neurology and Radiology at UCSF, and as a software engineering manager at Electrical Geodesics. More on the CV.
