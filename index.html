I work on noninvasive neuroimaging and neuromodulation, and on the open-source software that makes them reproducible. My background is in structural, diffusion, perfusion and functional MRI analysis at the Oxford FMRIB Analysis Group, King's College London, and UCSF, with earlier work in high-density EEG systems engineering. Current projects span developmental neurobiology, active inference, open lab hardware, and scientific packaging for Fedora.

Spinning Up

Module-by-module curricula, each a runnable repository.

  • spinning-up-alf — Spinning Up in Active Inference: 16 modules bridging reinforcement learning and active inference in JAX. Companion library: alf.
  • spinning-up-in-pde — Spinning Up in PDE Solvers: from finite differences to neural operators.
  • spinning-up-in-mech-interp — Spinning Up in Mechanistic Interpretability: a controls-first, open-weights curriculum from vision circuits to the global workspace.
  • spinning-up-in-openscope — Onboarding guide for the OpenScope Community Predictive-Processing project.

Orthogonal Research and Education Laboratory

  • controls-and-trajectories — The null distributions and developmental trajectories that interpretability papers rely on but rarely ship.
  • tri-lens — Do three interpretability instruments agree about the same activation? Jacobian lens, NLA and SAE, with mismatch nulls and steering as ground truth.
  • neurosim — NeuroSim: a JAX-native software stack for cognitive agent simulation.
  • SustainHub-jaxmarl — Pure-JAX multi-agent RL replication of the SustainHub open-source sustainability simulation (GSoC 2025).

OpenWorm / DevoWorm

  • hgx — Hypergraph neural networks in JAX/Equinox; started for the DevoWorm DevoGraph project (GSoC 2026).
  • devograph — Developmental neurobiology extensions for hgx: dynamics, geometric convolutions, optimal transport, and C. elegans tooling.
  • biohub-starter — Starter kit for the Biohub cell-tracking-during-development competition, with embryo-disjoint validation and negative results.

Biopunk Lab

  • anatomical-compiler — Engineering fully-biologic tissue systems: a hypergraph instrument for synthetic morphology, toward Levin's anatomical compiler.
  • biopunk-bioreactor — An evidence-based review of open-source continuous-culture bioreactors, with corrections and a two-instrument build proposal.
  • biopunk-jubilee — Closing the verification gap in Science Jubilee with physical-digital assertions and three sensors.
  • biopunk-duckweed — A DuckBot / OECD 221 duckweed bioassay pilot for Lake Wylie.
  • biopunk-artbot — Opentrons OT-2, bioprinter and flipdot display: pixel art to living bacterial art.
  • biopunk-display — Flipdot display server on a Raspberry Pi 4.
  • chimerax-vampnet — VAMPnet and Markov state modeling of protein conformational landscapes inside UCSF ChimeraX.
  • chimerax-origami — Off-target-aware DNA-origami sequence design and lipid-envelope modeling in ChimeraX.
